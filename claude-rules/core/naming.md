
General principles:
- Use descriptive names that reveal intent at the call site
- No single-letter names except loop counters (`i`, `j`, `k`); language-specific rules may extend this list
- No abbreviations except those universally understood (`api`, `db`, `http`, `id`, `params`, `props`, `url`, `utils`)
- Use one term per concept across the codebase. Pick one of `fetch`, `get`, or `load` for a given role and stay consistent
